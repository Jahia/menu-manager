First version: menus built by hand in jContent, independent of the page tree. A site holds as many menus as it needs, each a tree of pages (optionally with their subpages), sections, internal and external links, three levels deep; labels are translated per language and fall back to a fallback language, items can be switched off per language, and what a visitor may not read is left out of the menu they see.
